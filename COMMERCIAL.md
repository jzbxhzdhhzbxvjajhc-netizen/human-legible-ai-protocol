# Open adoption and future commercial value / 开放与商业边界

The protocol is opened to increase adoption, not to erase the founding project.

## What the public licenses allow

- Protocol material may be reused, adapted, shared, and used commercially under CC BY-SA 4.0, with attribution and share-alike obligations.
- The reference Skill and scripts may be used commercially under MPL 2.0; distributed modifications to covered files remain available under MPL 2.0.
- Rights already granted for a published version cannot simply be withdrawn later from people who follow the license.

This is the cost—and the distribution advantage—of an openly licensed protocol and open-source reference implementation. “Open source but competitors may not use it commercially” is not an open-source condition.

## What remains available for an official business

The project may later offer paid products and services such as:

- official conformance testing and certification;
- an official badge or verification service;
- enterprise implementation, migration, evaluation, and training;
- hosted tools, managed evaluation, and organization-specific rule packs;
- support, maintenance, and release guarantees;
- separate commercial terms for material the project has the legal right to license separately.

The protocol can remain free while trust, verification, convenience, implementation, and official project identity become commercial products.

## What others may not claim

The open licenses do not give anyone the right to claim that their fork, service, badge, or certification is official or endorsed. See `TRADEMARKS.md`.

## Before accepting any non-trivial outside contribution

Contributors retain copyright in their contributions and license them under the repository's applicable public license. That protects the commons, but any non-trivial or copyrightable contribution can limit the founding project's ability to relicense those contributions later without permission.

During the initial `0.x` stage, the project welcomes public Issues, Discussions, reviews, and proposed patches but pauses merging non-trivial outside contributions into normative protocol text or the reference implementation until the contribution-rights process has received appropriate legal review.

Before introducing paid commercial exceptions, a dual-license program, a contributor license agreement, or a registered certification mark, the project should obtain advice from a qualified intellectual-property lawyer in the relevant jurisdictions. Do not promise a future private relicense of community-owned contributions without that work.

## 中文结论

这个方案保住的是：**原始出处、同协议传播、官方版本、官方名称、官方认证和服务入口**。它不能保住“只有我能商用”，因为那与真正的开放协议冲突。

未来最稳的收入不是向所有人收“阅读协议费”，而是官方认证、企业落地、培训支持、托管工具和可信版本。若你更看重“别人商用必须付费”，就应在公开前改走“源码可见但非开源”的非商业许可路线；两条路线不能假装同时成立。
