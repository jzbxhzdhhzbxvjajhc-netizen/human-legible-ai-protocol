# Which AI should I open first?

[中文](first-ai-tools.zh-CN.md) · **English**

This is not a ranking of which AI is “most powerful.” It answers a more useful question: **which one is the easiest place to start with the problem in front of me?**

Here, an ordinary person means someone who can use a phone and computer but does not need to know words such as code, model, agent, workflow, deployment, or project.

If you have ever left an AI tool because you did not know how to ask—or feared your question was too basic—that is not your failure. You should not have to learn the tool's language before the tool explains why it matters to your life.

## Choose in one minute

| What you want to do now | Open this first | Think of it as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ask questions, learn, discuss an idea, or compare choices | ChatGPT | A general assistant that helps you think something through |
| Turn an idea into something usable on a computer, or keep working across a folder of files | Codex | A making partner that can enter a work folder, change things, and show you what it did |
| Organize office material, combine spreadsheets, build a report or slide deck, or start an office task from a phone | WorkBuddy | A work partner that breaks the job into steps, uses office tools, and hands back files |
| Ask a quick daily-life question, use voice or a photo, search, write, or edit an image in Chinese | Doubao mobile | An everyday assistant that is always close on a phone |

Open them here: [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) · [Codex](https://chatgpt.com/codex/) · [WorkBuddy](https://www.workbuddy.cn/) · [Doubao](https://www.doubao.com/). You will usually need an account. What appears can vary by plan, device, account, and region.

**If you are unsure, send the same sentence to any one of them:**

> I do not know AI terms or which feature to choose. The thing bothering me is: ____. First tell me whether you can help and what you need from me. Do not begin with technical terms.

## ChatGPT: talk it through, then turn it into useful material

### What is it, really?

Think of ChatGPT as a **general front desk**. You can describe a problem in everyday language. It can answer questions, explain, suggest ideas, compare options, and work from material you provide to produce documents, spreadsheets, or presentations.

The natural first step is not “create a project.” It can simply be:

> I keep forgetting to pay household bills. Help me design a reminder method I will actually follow.

> Explain inflation to my child using the price of groceries.

> I cannot understand these two insurance documents. First tell me which differences actually matter. Remind me to use a qualified professional before I buy anything.

### Open it first when

- you have not yet worked out what you really want;
- you want to ask, learn, discuss, or compare;
- you have several documents and want a summary or a clearer finished document;
- you want help turning a vague idea into a specific task.

### One important boundary

ChatGPT can do more than chat, but **an answer on screen does not prove that something happened in the outside world**. It may draft a complaint email; unless you clearly authorize sending and see that action happen, do not assume the email was sent.

Instead of asking “What features do you have?”, say:

> Every day I copy notices from three group chats into a spreadsheet. Which part of that hassle can you make easier?

## Codex: not a place where you must learn code, but a place where AI can keep working on files

### What is it, really?

Product pages often call Codex a coding agent. An ordinary user can start with this meaning:

> **Use Codex when the AI needs to do more than answer: open a group of files, change them, run checks, find a problem, and keep fixing it.**

Code is one material Codex handles well, but it can also work on spreadsheets, reports, slide decks, websites, and other results that can be saved and checked on a computer.

### What does “project” mean?

For now, understand it as:

> **Give this piece of work its own folder.**

“Daily income records for my barber shop” can be one project. “Organize old family photos” can be another. Keeping the related material together helps prevent it from being mixed with unrelated work.

### Open it first when

- you want to make a website, small tool, reusable spreadsheet, or other working result;
- you have many files to rename, combine, analyze, or maintain;
- the work will continue across several sessions instead of ending after one answer;
- you want the AI to check its work, not only tell you what you could do.

### Can I use it without knowing code?

Yes. Your job is to explain the real goal, limits, and what a useful result looks like:

> I run a barber shop. I want to enter only the number of visitors and the day's income, then see which days were strongest at the end of the month. Make a simple version I can open and use. I do not know code; explain every decision that affects me in everyday language.

You should decide whether the AI understood your routine, whether it may touch a folder, and whether the result feels right to use. The AI should choose technical methods and explain only the effects that matter to you.

Words you may see:

- **branch:** a separate change version that does not disturb the current one;
- **commit:** deliberately save a recovery point for the current result;
- **regression check:** check that a new change did not break something that already worked;
- **audit:** check what was done, why it was done, and whether anything is wrong; keeping a record makes that later check possible.

## WorkBuddy: hand over an office task and inspect the files it returns

### What is it, really?

WorkBuddy focuses on taking an office goal, breaking it into steps, using suitable tools, and returning a report, spreadsheet, slide deck, or another result you can inspect.

For example:

> Combine these three sales spreadsheets, identify products that declined for two months, and return one conclusion page plus a spreadsheet I can continue editing. Do not change the originals.

> Research how this industry changed over the last year. Give sources for important claims and return a slide deck I can present in ten minutes.

### What is WorkBuddy on a phone?

The useful mental model is not “a whole computer squeezed onto a small screen.” It is:

> **The phone is a remote control for giving a task, adding material, checking progress, and deciding whether work should continue.**

Official descriptions distinguish work running on a provider-hosted computer from work remotely arranged on your own WorkBuddy computer. The mobile entry can accept text, voice, images, and files. Exact features differ across the iPhone, Android, HarmonyOS, and mini-program entries, so trust what your own account actually shows.

### Open it first when

- the result is mainly a document, spreadsheet, slide deck, report, or organized office material;
- the task crosses several office tools and you want less copying and pasting;
- you want to send the task from a phone and inspect the result later;
- your work already lives in Tencent office products and moving less data between tools saves time.

### A safe first task

“It can act on its own” does not mean “give it everything.” For important material:

1. provide a copy, not the only original;
2. allow only the folder required for the task;
3. ask for a small sample before a large batch;
4. require a pause before sending, publishing, deleting, paying, or changing access;
5. inspect both the result and the actions taken.

## Doubao mobile: a Chinese everyday entry point on your phone

### What is it, really?

Think of Doubao mobile as an everyday Chinese-language assistant suited to quick use. You can type, speak, take a photo, or give it a file for explanation, search, writing, translation, study help, planning, image editing, or some office work.

For example:

> I am at a hospital and cannot understand this process chart. Explain only the steps; do not diagnose me.

> I have eggs, tomatoes, and half a cabbage. Plan two simple meals without asking me to buy ten spices.

> Read this photographed notice. Tell me the deadline, what I need to bring, and the first thing I should do today.

### Open it first when

- you are away from a computer;
- speaking or taking a photo is easier than typing;
- the task concerns daily life, study, Chinese writing, search, or images;
- you want a useful answer now and do not need a long-lived work folder.

Doubao can also work with documents, slide decks, and spreadsheets, so these products are not separated by absolute “can” and “cannot” lines. The main difference is the easiest entry and the situation each product is designed around.

### Protect what a phone camera can reveal

Convenience makes it easy to photograph an identity card, address, medical record, contract, child information, or company material by accident. Before sending, ask:

> Would I still send this if an outside service had to process the image?

If unsure, cover names, numbers, addresses, and any detail the task does not require. AI can help explain medical, legal, or investment material and prepare questions, but it should not be the only basis for a high-stakes final decision.

## One problem, four starting points

Suppose your problem is: **every month, your household cannot tell where the money went.**

ChatGPT:

> I have never kept a budget and will not follow a complicated method. Ask me three questions, then suggest a method that takes less than one minute a day.

Codex:

> Turn the method we chose into a household budget sheet I can keep using. Do not change my original bank statements. Check the formulas and tell me exactly where to enter each day's information.

WorkBuddy:

> Combine copies of my bank and payment statements, group this month's spending into food, travel, housing, shopping, and other, and make a report. Put anything uncertain in a separate list for me to decide.

Doubao mobile:

> Read this grocery receipt, turn the items and total into one record, and tell me how much I spent today.

## The tools overlap—how do I choose?

All four can chat, and several can search, read files, write documents, or make images. You do not need a permanent product map. Ask three questions:

1. Am I trying to think something through, or do I need it to produce or modify a real file?
2. Is the material mainly on my phone, inside one computer folder, or spread across office tools?
3. If this goes wrong, is it easy to undo? If not, use copies, narrow access, and inspect every step.

If one tool already completes the task comfortably, do not switch only because another is described as more professional.

## One instruction that works with any AI

> Assume I can use a phone and computer but do not know AI or software-industry vocabulary. First tell me in everyday language what problem you are helping me reduce, what you need from me, which actions would change or send my things, and where I need to decide. Remove technical terms when possible; if one must appear, explain it immediately. Do not hand technical choices back to me. Ask me only about choices that affect price, time, privacy, risk, or how the result feels to use.
