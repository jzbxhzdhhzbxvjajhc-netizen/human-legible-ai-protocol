# Project name and official-status policy / 项目名称与官方身份

This policy concerns project identity. It does not change the copyright licenses.

`Human-Legible AI Protocol`, `HLAI Protocol`, `AI 人话协议`, the repository's official logos, and official conformance or certification badges are identifiers of the canonical project. They are not offered under the content or code licenses.

## Allowed without separate permission

- truthful statements such as “implements Human-Legible AI Protocol v0.2.0”;
- linking to, discussing, reviewing, or criticizing the project;
- retaining required attribution and license notices;
- naming a compatible implementation in a way that clearly identifies its independent source, for example “Acme Translator — compatible with HLAI Protocol v0.2.0”.

## Requires written permission

- using “official”, “certified”, “verified”, or equivalent language that implies project approval;
- using an official logo or badge as product branding;
- naming a fork or service in a way that could reasonably be mistaken for the canonical project;
- registering a confusingly similar domain, account, organization, product, or certification mark.

During the `0.x` stage, written permission can be issued only by the founding steward identified in `GOVERNANCE.md`, through a publicly verifiable repository record or a separately signed agreement.

Modified or non-conforming forks must use a distinct primary name and state that they are independent. They may still provide truthful attribution and compatibility information.

No registered-trademark claim is made by this file. Rights and enforceability vary by jurisdiction. Registration and any paid certification program require separate legal review.

## 中文一句话

你可以诚实地说“兼容 AI 人话协议”，但不能把自己的分叉、课程、认证或产品包装成“官方”。名称和官方标识的作用，是让别人能使用协议，却不能轻易冒充协议的源头。
